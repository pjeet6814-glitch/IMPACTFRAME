IMPACTFRAME is a student media club that turns environmental conservation into short films, AI-made visual poems, and investigative documentaries — made by students, for anyone who still wants to save something.

[ 02 // ETHOS ]

Conserve

Every single shoot starts from an ecological reality — campus borewells drying out, rooftop heat islands, or local river degradation.

[ 03 // ACTION ]

We screen on campus lawns, submit to international eco-film festivals, and distribute online so our stories lead to tangible action.
