[ CLUB MANIFESTO & DEPARTMENTS ]

Cinema is how conservation becomes unavoidable.

Scientific publications track our drying water tables and kilowatt spikes. But numbers alone rarely change habits — visceral cinema does.

"We do not shoot glossy corporate nature ads. We film the campus dormitories where air conditioners hum through empty hallways at 3 AM, the cracked irrigation canals bordering rural farms, and the student-built drone sensors measuring urban heat islands. If a frame doesn't tell the unvarnished truth about what we are losing, it belongs on the cutting floor."

[ PRODUCTION SPECIALIZATIONS ]

Our Creative Departments

How student directors, field engineers, and prompt designers collaborate from pre-production to international festival screenings.

DIRECTION & SCREENWRITING

Narrative & Investigative Journalism

Conducts field research with faculty professors, fact-checks energy and groundwater metrics, and scripts human-centered stories where ecological conservation is felt intimately.

FIELD CINEMATOGRAPHY

Anamorphic Glass & Aerial 4K Mapping

Operates cinema rigs, anamorphic glass, and mapping drones through rain, dust storms, rural wetlands, and nocturnal electrical surveys across campus infrastructure.

FOLEY & SPATIAL SOUND

Tactile Acoustic Design & Hydrophones

Using parabolic dishes and submersible hydrophones to record the acoustic signature of vanishing ecosystems: subsurface water suction, cicada colonies, and electrical transformers.

AI GENERATIVE LAB

Speculative Ecological Visuals

Leveraging video diffusion and neural models to render what cannot normally be filmed: reconstructing prehistoric flora or visualizing 2050 climatic projection models with scientific rigor.

WORKFLOW

Production Workflow

How our student crew collaborates from concept to campus premiere.

PHASE 01

Research & Ideation

Identifying local environmental themes, conducting interviews, and developing narrative or documentary scripts.

PHASE 02

Field Production

On-location shooting across campus and surrounding regions with student camera operators, sound recordists, and directors.

PHASE 03

Post-Production

Editing, audio assembly, foley sync, and visual grading carried out collaboratively by student team members.

PHASE 04

Campus Screenings

Showcasing completed projects at open amphitheater screenings, film discussions, and university digital platforms.
