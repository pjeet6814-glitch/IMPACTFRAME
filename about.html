[ CLUB MANIFESTO & DEPARTMENTS ]

Cinema is how conservation becomes unavoidable.

Scientific papers detail our water depletion and carbon output, but numbers rarely keep anyone awake at night. We turn research into rhythm, lens flares, and human consequence.

"We don't shoot glossy nature promos. We document the hostel corridors where lights burn at 4 AM, the dry riverbed beneath the highway overpass, and the students building drone sensors to measure rooftop heat islands. If a shot doesn't tell the truth about what we are losing, it belongs on the cutting floor."

Our Creative Departments

How our student directors, field engineers, and prompt designers collaborate from pre-production to festival submissions.

DIRECTION & WRITING

Narrative & Investigative Journalism

Conducts field research, fact-checks environmental audits with campus professors, and scripts human-centered stories where conservation is personal, not preachy.

FIELD CINEMATOGRAPHY

Camera & Aerial Mapping

Shooting on mirrorless cinema rigs and 4K mapping drones. Rain, dust, muddy riverbanks, and night thermal passes across campus infrastructure.

FOLEY & SOUND DESIGN

Tactile Field Audio & Spatial Sound

Using parabolic reflectors and hydrophones to capture the actual acoustic heartbeat of disappearing ecosystems: cracked clay, cicadas, electrical humming.

AI GENERATIVE LAB

Ecological Speculative Cinema

Harnessing video diffusion and generative neural tools to visualize the invisible: visualizing what a campus forest looked like in 1890, or forecasting 2050 climate scenarios.
