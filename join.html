Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you wanted to ask before signing up.

Do I need my own DSLR or cinema camera?

No. All cameras, anamorphic prime lenses, drone rigs, and field microphones are checked out directly from our collective club gear locker.

I'm a first-year student. Can I still direct?

Absolutely. Every production pairs passionate freshmen with experienced senior editors so you learn on actual field sets.

How much time commitment is required?

Typically 3–5 hours a week during active shoots, plus weekend screening workshops. We schedule shoots entirely around exams and class timetables.

Can I submit AI-generated films?

Yes, as long as they explore genuine ecological questions, local biodiversity, or speculative energy scenarios.
