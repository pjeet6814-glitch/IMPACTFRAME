Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you wanted to ask before signing up.

Do I need my own DSLR or cinema camera?

No. All cameras, prime lenses, drone setups, and field microphones are checked out from our collective club gear locker.

I'm a first-year student. Can I still direct?

Absolutely. Every project pairs newcomers with experienced senior editors so you learn on actual production sets.

How much time commitment is required?

Typically 3–5 hours a week during active shoots, plus weekend screening workshops. We schedule around midterms and exams.

Can I submit AI-generated films?

Yes, as long as they engage with real ecological questions, energy conservation, or speculative climate forecasting.
